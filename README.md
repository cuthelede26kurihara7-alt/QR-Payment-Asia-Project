# QR-Payment-Asia-Project
Data journalism project analyzing digital payment adoption in Asia using Global Findex data.
# Beyond QR Codes: Digital Payment Infrastructure in Asia

## Project Overview

QR payments are often viewed as a technology story. However, successful digital payment adoption depends on deeper financial infrastructure, including access to financial services, mobile connectivity, and merchant payment ecosystems.

This project analyzes digital payment readiness in Southeast Asia using Global Findex 2024 data and explores how mobile financial access connects to digital merchant payment adoption.

The goal is to understand why some countries have developed stronger digital payment ecosystems and how QR payments can improve business agility for small businesses.

---

## Research Questions

1. Which Asian countries show stronger digital payment adoption?
2. How does mobile financial access relate to digital merchant payment usage?
3. How can QR payment ecosystems create opportunities for small businesses?

---

## Data Source

**World Bank Global Findex Database 2024**

Source:
https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex

Key variables analyzed:

* `account_t_d`

  * Ownership of a financial account

* `mobileaccount_t_d`

  * Ownership of a mobile financial account

* `merchant_pay`

  * Adults who made digital payments to merchants

## Methodology

Data analysis was conducted using Python and Jupyter Notebook.

Workflow:

1. Load Global Findex dataset
2. Select relevant financial inclusion indicators
3. Filter Asian countries
4. Calculate digital payment readiness indicators
5. Create visualizations using Datawrapper
6. Publish results through GitHub Pages

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Digital payment adoption varies significantly across countries

Malaysia and Thailand show higher levels of digital merchant payment usage compared with Indonesia and the Philippines.

This suggests that QR payment adoption depends not only on technology availability but also on financial infrastructure and user behavior.

### 2. Mobile financial access supports digital payment ecosystems

Countries with stronger mobile financial access tend to show higher digital merchant payment adoption.

Mobile financial infrastructure can act as a foundation for broader digital commerce.

### 3. QR payments and business agility

QR payments can reduce barriers for small businesses by lowering the need for expensive payment infrastructure.

Digital payment ecosystems allow businesses to participate in digital commerce with fewer operational constraints.
